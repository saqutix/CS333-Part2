All charts use transactions registered from 1 January 2015 to 31 August 2026 (both included). Dates are handled in UTC, so the results do not depend on the viewer's time zone.

1. Weekly Transaction Patterns

Chart 1 will appear here.

Answer to Question 1

6,553 transactions were registered on Fridays between 1 January 2015 and 31 August 2026. The bar chart shows all seven days from Monday to Sunday, each in its own color. Monday is the busiest day (20,126 transactions), Friday is the lowest of the days with transactions, and Saturday and Sunday have zero transactions in this dataset. They are still shown on the x-axis (with empty bars) so the full week is visible. Hover over any bar to see its exact count.

Answer to Question 2

September 2025 has the highest number of off-plan sales, with 706 sales. This was found by comparing monthly off-plan sales across all 140 months of the window (January 2015 – August 2026). The next highest months are October 2024 (665), December 2025 (656) and July 2025 (650), so the peak is clear. Hover over any point to see the month, property type and number of sales.

3. Dubai Community Price Map

Chart 3 will appear here.
How to read the map. Blue shades show the median sale price per m² of units and villas in each community. Because median prices are strongly skewed (most communities are between about 7,000 and 19,000 AED/m², while a few reach 50,000 to 75,000), the colors use quantile classes: each of the 7 shades covers roughly the same number of communities, so differences among the many mid-priced communities remain visible and the few very expensive ones do not wash everything else out. Grey means the community has no sales, or fewer than 10 sales, of units and villas. A median based on so few sales is not reliable, so it is not colored (the tooltip still shows the number of sales and the median). The map is zoomed on the urban area of Dubai; the remote desert communities outside the frame (for example Hatta and Hefair) are part of the data but have no or very few sales, so they would be grey in any case.

Supporting chart: communities with the most sales

Answers to Question 3

Al Barsha South Fourth (community 681) has the largest number of unit and villa sales, with 3,925 sales (median price about 12,923 AED/m²). It is followed by Business Bay (3,634) and Marsa Dubai (2,955).

Is the map effective for finding it? No. The color encodes the median price, not the number of sales, so the map cannot show which community has the most sales. The reader would have to hover over each of the 113 communities with sales and compare the counts in the tooltips by memory. The area of a polygon is also unrelated to its sales volume: large communities look prominent but may have few sales, while small coastal communities with many sales are hard to see. Tooltips make the answer possible but not effective. A bar chart sorted in descending order (shown above the answers) answers the question immediately, because length is a much more accurate visual channel than color for comparing counts. The map is effective for its own purpose, comparing how expensive different parts of the city are.
